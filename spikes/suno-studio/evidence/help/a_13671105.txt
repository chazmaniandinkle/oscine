 In this article How to use it Home Studio Studio 2.0 Remove FX (Get Dry) in Studio Edited Thursday, August 13 2026 Remove FX gives you the dry version of a sound. It uses AI to strip out reverb, delay and other effects baked into the audio, which makes it perfect for cleaning up a sound before you shape it with your own effects. How to use it Right-click the header of the audio clip you want to remove fx from. Choose Remove FX from the dropdown menu. The dry version lands as a new take lane and auto-auditions in place. Commit it if you like what you hear.
