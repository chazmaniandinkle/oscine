 Home Studio Studio Archive Suno Studio (v1) Introducing Studio v1.1 Edited Friday, November 14 2025 Studio v1.1 is here! Studio's first update, and it's packed with some great new updates to get you making your best music possible: Track EQ (Equalizer) - Now you can fine-tune and shape the sounds of your tracks directly in Studio with the introduction of Studio's new 6-band equalizer. This professional-grade EQ gives you complete control over 6 distinct frequency bands as well as 6 bell curves, allowing you to sculpt your audio with precision. Read more about it here. Loop Recording - Loop Recording in Suno Studio enables you to capture multiple takes by continuously recording over the same time range, making it ideal for perfecting performances through repetition and creating polished compositions from your best moments. You can then access all your takes through the Show Take Lanes icon to comp together the strongest parts from different passes to edit and combine clips into a final polished track. Read more about Loop Recording here! Context Window - The new Context Window will allow you to hone in on specific parts of your project that you want use to influence what you generate. Content before or after a selected region will be ignored when creating new clips. Redesigned Project Library - We've redesigned the Project Library for easier access to your Studio projects. You'll able to search through your Project Library and access recently deleted projects.
