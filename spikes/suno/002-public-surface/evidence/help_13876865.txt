Privacy Policy Reject Accept Home Rights & Ownership Rights How do downloads work? Edited Thursday, September 3 2026 We built Suno for creating, sharing, and listening. In addition to doing all of that directly in the app or on our website, some creators may find it helpful to download their songs. Currently: Pro plan subscribers will receive 20 download credits per month. Premier plan subscribers will receive 60 download credits per month. Workflows in Suno Studio will not be subject to download limits. Free users who have joined before September 3, 2026 will have up to 7 trial downloads with the ability to purchase additional downloads. Free users who join on or after September 3, 2026 may occasionally receive trial downloads, and will also have the ability to purchase downloads. Trial downloads are not eligible for commercial use.
