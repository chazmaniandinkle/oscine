Privacy Policy Reject Accept Home Making Music How do I download songs? Edited Wednesday, September 9 2026 You can download your songs from your Library or Workspace. Download a single song 1. Find the song you want to download 2. Select the More Actions (...) menu 3. Hover over or select Download 4. Choose your preferred download option Download multiple songs 1. Hold Command on Mac or Ctrl on Windows while selecting individual songs 2. Hold Shift to select a range of songs 3. Right-click one of the selected songs 4. Select Download All 5. Choose your file type and select Download Your selected songs will be downloaded together as a ZIP file.
