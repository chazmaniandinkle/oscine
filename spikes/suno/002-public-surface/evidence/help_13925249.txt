Privacy Policy Reject Accept Home Making Music Exporting from Studio Edited Wednesday, September 9 2026 Studio offers flexible export options to help you get your music out of Suno and into your production workflow. Using the Export Dropdown Menu The Export dropdown is in the top-right above the Timeline. • Full Song — Exports the complete mix of your project. • Selected Time Range — Exports only the portion of the timeline you've selected. • Multitrack — Exports all tracks as stems for mixing in your DAW of choice. Exporting Individual Clips 1. Right-click any audio clip on the timeline. 2. Select Download .WAV from the context menu. Exporting MIDI from Stems 1. Extract stems from your clip using the Stems panel. 2. Click on the stem you want. 3. Select Get MIDI (costs 10 credits). 4. Studio generates a MIDI file you can import into any DAW.
