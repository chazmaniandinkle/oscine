Privacy Policy Reject Accept Home Making Music How to Use: Audio Uploads Edited Friday, May 30 2025 Audio uploads can be an integral process of creating in Suno. Whether you've recorded a rough demo and want to instantly bring a song to life, or heard something interesting on a walk and want to remember it in a special way, audio uploads can transform any moment into a song. Creators on the Basic (free) plan can upload up to 60 seconds of audio, then Extend , Cover , etc. Subscribers with Pro or Premier plans can now upload up to 8 minutes , allowing you to upload full songs, rather than just parts. In the Create page, the Audio Upload button is located directly next to the Create button: Click the button to record or add an existing audio file. The audio will be added to your library so you can create something new with it at anytime!
