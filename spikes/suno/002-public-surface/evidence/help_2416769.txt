Edited Wednesday, January 7 2026 We know that ownership of your material is important, and it's helpful to know exactly where you stand before you take the next steps with your songs! The easiest way to know would be to ask yourself "was I subscribed when I made the song?" If you were subscribed with a Pro or Premier plan when the song was created, you are considered the owner of the song. You also retain the rights to commercial use for the song, even if you end your subscription. If you are using the free version of Suno (our Basic tier), we retain ownership of the songs you generate, but you are allowed to use those songs for non-commercial purposes, subject to your compliance with Suno’s Terms of Service . It might not be the most thrilling to read, but you'll almost always find the answers you need in the Terms of Service .
