Edited Wednesday, September 9 2026 Stems let you break a song into separate musical parts — vocals, drums, bass, guitar, keys, and more — so you can work with each one individually. From your Workspace or Library 1. Select the More Actions (...) menu next to any song 2. Select Get Stems 3. Choose a split type 4. Select Extract > Note: Get Stems is available to Pro and Premier subscribers. From Suno Studio 1. Right-click an audio clip on the timeline 2. Select Split Stems 3. Choose a split type 4. Select Extract What is Advanced Stem Separation? Advanced Stem Separation breaks a song down with more precision and cleaner results, with three modes: Auto Split — Separates into up to 12 stems. Available on Pro and Premier. Costs 50 credits per extraction. Split from Mix — Extracts a single instrument or vocal, plus a complement track. Available on Pro and Premier. Costs 10 credits per stem. Advanced Split — You choose which stems to separate from a list of nearly 100 instruments. Premier only. Costs 10 credits per stem. > Note: Advanced Split is available to Premier subscribers only.
